Part 2: WOW! Travel Blog

L

Lara's Travel Tips ✈️

Posted 3 minutes ago

Travelling is fun, but lots of things can go wrong. Read my travel tips and you'll have a good trip!

First, remember the important things like your passport, ticket, and money. Mum usually takes those for me, but check the date on your passport! Children need a new passport every 5 years, but parents only change theirs every 10 years, so they sometimes forget!

Use a rucksack (backpack) or a suitcase on wheels. If you put your name and address on it, you won't lose it. Take shorts and flip-flops for sunny days 😎. Take a raincoat or an umbrella for rainy days.

Take a washbag with your toothbrush, toothpaste, shampoo and soap. And don't forget medicines if you need them!

Don't take heavy guidebooks; you can download apps about the places you're going to visit. But remember to take a charger for your phone! If you're travelling at night, take a pillow so you can sleep. Have a good trip! 😊

📝 Reading Check

1. How often do children need a new passport?

✅ Answer: Every 5 years.

2. Why shouldn't you take guidebooks?

✅ Answer: They are heavy (you can download apps).

3. What do you need if you travel at night?

✅ Answer: A pillow so you can sleep.

🎨 Group Project: Travel Do's & Don'ts!

Work in groups of 3. Grab some paper and markers! Design a "Travel Survival Guide" poster with 3 Do's (Good ideas) and 3 Don'ts (Bad ideas). Present it to the class!

✅ DO (Good Ideas)
  • 👉 "Do check your passport date."
  • 👉 "Do take a charger."
  • 👉 "Do try the local food!"
❌ DON'T (Bad Ideas)
  • 👉 "Don't take heavy guidebooks."
  • 👉 "Don't forget your medicine."
  • 👉 "Don't lose your ticket."

💡 Presentation Tips:

Make it colorful! Everyone in the group should speak. Stand tall, smile, and show your poster to the class.

"Hello everyone! Here are our travel tips. First, you should DO..."

Part 3: The Future Crossroads

📝

Going To

"The Planner"

Use this when you have already decided to do something before speaking. It's a plan in your head!

✓ "I'm going to visit Madrid."

✓ "We're going to travel in July."

💡

Will ('ll)

"The Hero"

Use this when you decide right now at the time of speaking. Great for offers, promises & quick ideas.

A: "I don't have an umbrella."

B: "Don't worry, I'll take one!"
